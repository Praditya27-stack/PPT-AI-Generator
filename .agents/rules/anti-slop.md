# Anti-AI-Slop Presentation Standards

## 1. Copywriting Principles (Minto Pyramid / SCQA)
- **Action-Oriented Headlines**:
  - BAD: "Digital Marketing in 2026", "Introduction to Cloud"
  - GOOD: "Scaling Revenue 3x Through Automated Acquisition", "Zero-Trust Cloud Architecture for Distributed Teams"
  - Headlines must be max 8–10 words and provide an opinionated insight.
- **Concise Body Statements**:
  - Maximum 15 words per bullet or card description.
  - Ban corporate fluff words ("synergy", "paradigm shift", "harnessing power", "journey into future").
  - Focus on concrete mechanisms: what, why, and the measured outcome.

## 2. Visual Layout Requirements
Every generated presentation deck must utilize a balanced variety of layouts:
- `hero`: Title, subtitle, topic badge, and presenter/organization note.
- `cards`: 2-3 distinct container cards with structured insight (title, description, icon concept).
- `stats`: 2-3 big bold metrics (`value` like "78%", "3.4x", "<50ms") + concise label and rationale.
- `timeline`: 3-4 chronological or procedural stages (Step 1 -> Step 2 -> Step 3).
- `callout`: Single high-impact quote, thesis statement, or critical takeaway with visual accent.

## 3. Theme & Typography Rules
- Default theme is modern dark mode (`#0B0F19` deep slate base, `#06B6D4` cyan or `#10B981` emerald accents).
- High visual contrast: Primary text white (`#F8FAFC`), secondary text muted slate (`#94A3B8`).
- Slide dimensions: Standard 16:9 widescreen ratio.
